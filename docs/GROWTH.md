# Tally — growth engine

Virality is a product surface, not a marketing afterthought. Build these with the same care as the research itself. Guardrail (absolute, restated from `CLAUDE.md`): no growth mechanic may compromise independence, add purchase pressure, or degrade the advice. When a viral feature and trust conflict, trust wins.

## Shareable reports — the primary loop

- Every completed report has a **public, read-only share page** at a clean, stable URL. No login to view. Fast, editorial-grade on mobile, carrying the full verdict, tradeoffs, comparison grid, and sources.
- Share pages render rich **Open Graph / social cards**: product image, verdict line, Tally wordmark, one memorable tradeoff. The card alone should be worth a screenshot.
- Every share page has one prominent, low-friction call to action — **"Research your own — free"** — leading to the search box pre-warmed with a related example query. This visitor→searcher conversion is the loop's engine; measure it.
- Shared pages are dated snapshots with a visible research date and a "prices may have changed — re-run this research" action that starts a fresh session for the visitor.

## Screenshot-ready verdicts

Design the verdict block, comparison grid, and pros/cons card so a phone screenshot of any one of them is legible, attributed ("Researched by Tally"), and complete enough to settle a group-chat argument. Screenshots are a major distribution channel; design for them deliberately.

## Decide-with-friends polls

- From any shortlist, the user can create a **decision poll**: a shareable link where friends vote on finalists and leave one-line takes. Voters see the evidence, not just the horse race.
- No account required to vote or to research; accounts (if any) gate only saved history.
- Frame around the real social moment: "Can't decide? Let your people weigh in."

## Public research library — the SEO loop

- High-quality completed research on popular queries — with the researcher's consent, stripped of personal assumptions — feeds an indexable library: canonical pages like "Best cordless vacuum for pet hair — researched [date]" showing methodology, sources, and freshness, funneling to personalized research.
- Freshness is a trust feature: show research dates prominently; re-run stale high-traffic pages automatically.

## Retention hooks that respect the user

- **Price watch:** one tap on any recommended product to be notified of a price drop or a shipping successor — the honest version of urgency: real information, on the user's terms.
- **Post-purchase check-in:** weeks later, "Did the pick work out?" One tap. Closes the learning loop and creates a genuine reason to return.
- Never: streaks, fake scarcity, countdown pressure, notification spam. Every re-engagement touch must contain real information value.

## Instrumentation

Instrument every surface in this file: share taps, link visits, social-card impressions where measurable, visitor→searcher conversion, poll creation and participation, price-watch sets and notification outcomes, library page traffic and conversion. The viral loop must be measurable end to end or it cannot be improved.
